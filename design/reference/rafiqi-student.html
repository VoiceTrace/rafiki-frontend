Newton's Third Law

Action & Reaction

The idea — pick the format that works for you

For every action there is an equal and opposite reaction. When object A pushes object B, B pushes A back with exactly the same force in the opposite direction — no matter how different their size or weight.

Equal arrows — same force, opposite direction
